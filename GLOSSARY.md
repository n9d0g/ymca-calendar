# YMCA Calendar

A subscribable calendar of a YMCA Centre's schedule, so its Sessions show up in a personal calendar app instead of being looked up on the YMCA website.

## Language

### The schedule

**Centre**:
A single YMCA facility. Only the McDonald Family YMCA Centre is in scope.
_Avoid_: Location, branch, gym, club

**Room**:
The space inside a Centre where a Session takes place, e.g. Studio, Pool, or "Gym 1 and 2" when a Session uses more than one space.
_Avoid_: Location, area, facility

**Program**:
A named activity the Centre runs repeatedly, e.g. Aquafit.
_Avoid_: Class, activity, course

**Session**:
One scheduled occurrence of a Program, at a specific date and time, in a Room, run by a Leader.
_Avoid_: Class, event, occurrence, slot

**Leader**:
The person running a Session.
_Avoid_: Instructor, coach, teacher, trainer

**Category**:
The YMCA's grouping of Programs. There are five: Group Fitness, Aquatics, Adult Recreational Sports, Child Youth and Family Programs, and Community and Leadership.
_Avoid_: Type, department, program type

**Sign-up**:
A booking members must make for a single Session, usually opening shortly before it starts. Marked ⓢ by the YMCA.
_Avoid_: Booking, reservation

**Registration**:
Enrolment in a Program for its whole run, made in advance. Marked ⓡ by the YMCA.
_Avoid_: Enrolment, sign-up

### The calendar

**Feed**:
A subscribable calendar holding the Sessions of one Category.
_Avoid_: Calendar, ICS, subscription
