AquaFit (Combo) ⓢ

YMCA AquaFit Combo class is designed for all ages and abilities! This 45-minute session offers a safe yet challenging workout that combines low-impact cardio, muscle conditioning, and stretching—all set to upbeat music that keeps the energy high. You will enjoy a dynamic, fun-filled experience that helps you stay active while protecting your joints. Participants choose to work out in the shallow end of the pool or in deep water with the support of a floatation belt. This flexibility allows everyone to exercise at their own comfort level. Join us for a lively and invigorating class that will leave you feeling refreshed, strong, and energized! Your sign-up window begins 24 hours before class.
